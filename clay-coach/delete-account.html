Delete your Clay Coach account

Clay Coach by ROPH Studios

How to ask

Email hello@rophstudios.com from the email address you sign in to Clay Coach with, with the subject "Delete my Clay Coach account". We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and account ID
  • Your rounds and scores
  • Your private courses and your favourite clubs

What may be kept

Clubs and layouts you added to the shared club directory may stay so that other users can keep using them, with the link to your account removed. Nothing that identifies you is kept.

More detail is in the Clay Coach privacy policy.
