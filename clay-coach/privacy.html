Clay Coach: Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

In short: Clay Coach stores your sign-in details and the rounds, courses and favourites you create, so they are there on any device you sign in on. We don't sell your data, show ads or use analytics, and you can have everything deleted at any time.

Who we are

Clay Coach is made by ROPH Studios, the trading name of Tobias Backer Dirks, an independent developer based in Cyprus, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: hello@rophstudios.com.

What we collect

  • Your account. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a unique account ID from Google. We use them only to sign you in and to keep your data linked to you.
  • What you record. Your rounds and scores, the courses and layouts you set up, and the clubs you mark as favourites.
  • What you share. Clubs, layouts and courses you make public are added to a shared directory that other Clay Coach users can see. They are linked to your account ID, but your name and email are not shown with them.

We do not collect your location, contacts or device identifiers, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: signing you in, saving and syncing what you record, and showing the shared club directory. The legal basis is providing the service you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), and, for anything you choose to share publicly, our legitimate interest in keeping a useful shared directory (Article 6(1)(f)).

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it on our behalf. Google may store it on servers outside your country, including outside the EU, under the safeguards described in Firebase's privacy and security information. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When your account is deleted, your sign-in details, rounds, private courses and favourites are deleted. Clubs and layouts you added to the shared directory may stay so that other users can keep using them, with the link to your account removed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it is used, by emailing hello@rophstudios.com. To delete your account and data, see Delete your Clay Coach account. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Cyprus that is the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

Children

Clay Coach is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
