Clay Coach: Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

In short: Clay Coach stores your account and the rounds, courses and favourites you create, so they are there on any device you sign in on. Your location is used only to find clubs near you and is never saved. We don't sell your data, show ads or use analytics, and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Clay Coach is made by ROPH Studios, the trading name of Tobias Backer Dirks, an independent developer based in Cyprus, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: hello@rophstudios.com.

What we collect

  • Your account. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and a unique account ID from Google. If you sign up with an email address, we store that address; your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and we never see it. We use these only to sign you in and to keep your data linked to you.
  • What you record. Your rounds: where and when you shot, the targets, your hits and misses, and, if you turn it on, which way you missed. Also the clubs you mark as favourites. Your rounds are private to you.
  • What you add to the shared directory. Clubs, courses and layouts you add or edit are part of a directory that other Clay Coach users can see and use. Courses and layouts you create are linked to your account ID: only you can change or delete a course you created, and only you can delete a layout you created, though other users can update it to keep it current. Your name and email are not shown with them. A course you mark as private is visible only to you.
  • Settings on your device. A few preferences, such as whether to record miss direction, are saved on the device only and never sent to us.

Location

If you allow it, Clay Coach uses your device's approximate location to show clubs near you and how far away they are. The app uses it on the device to look up clubs in the surrounding area; your location is not saved, not linked to your account and not shared. You can use Clay Coach without allowing location and search for clubs by name or place instead.

Maps

Maps are loaded from OpenFreeMap, using OpenStreetMap data. To show a map, your device requests map images for the area on screen directly from OpenFreeMap, which, like any website, sees your IP address when it serves them. No account information is sent.

Purchases

Clay Coach Premium is a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase, including payment, is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so that it can unlock Premium; we do not receive your payment details.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: signing you in, saving and syncing what you record, coaching you from your own rounds, and running the shared club directory. The legal basis is providing the service you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), and, for what you add to the shared directory, our legitimate interest in keeping a useful directory for all users (Article 6(1)(f)). Location is used only when you allow it (Article 6(1)(a)), and you can withdraw that permission in your device settings at any time.

Where it is stored

Your account and data are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes them on our behalf. Google may store them on servers outside your country, including outside the EU, under the safeguards described in Firebase's privacy and security information. Apart from that, we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. You can delete your account in the app under Settings, Delete account & data. That immediately deletes your sign-in details, your rounds, your favourites and your private courses (with their layouts). Public courses and layouts you added stay in the shared directory so that other users can keep using them, with the link to your account removed. Clubs are not linked to the user who added them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it is used, by emailing hello@rophstudios.com. If you can't sign in to delete your account yourself, email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete it within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Cyprus that is the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

Children

Clay Coach is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also say so in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@rophstudios.com. See also the Terms of Use.
