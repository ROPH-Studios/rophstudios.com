Furniture Store Games: Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

In short: Furniture Store Games does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Furniture Store Games is made by ROPH Studios, the trading name of Tobias Backer Dirks, an independent developer based in Cyprus. You can reach us at hello@rophstudios.com.

Information we collect

None. The game has no sign-in and does not ask for a name, email address, photos, location or any other personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party tracking code.

What stays on the device

The game saves a few settings on the device itself: music and sound-effect volume, and whether the full game has been unlocked. This never leaves the device, and deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

The full game can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase, including payment, is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so that it can unlock the content; we do not receive your payment details. Before any purchase, the game shows a grown-up check so that a child cannot start one alone.

Internet access

The game asks for internet access only so it can talk to Google Play for the purchase and for restoring it. Everything else works offline.

Children

The game is made for young children. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either. It does not contain ads or links that leave the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
