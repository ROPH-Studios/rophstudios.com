Furniture Store Games

For young children · Android · No ads

Inspired by the play corners in furniture stores: four simple games for small hands.

  • Build: put a table, chair, lamp, bookshelf and sofa together, piece by piece.
  • Memory: turn over cards to find the pairs.
  • Puzzle: finish a picture jigsaw.
  • Paint: pick a colour and a brush and paint freely.

The first set in each game is free. A single one-time purchase unlocks everything, with no subscriptions and no ads. The game collects no personal information: privacy policy.
