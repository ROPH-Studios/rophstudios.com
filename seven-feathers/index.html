Seven Feathers

For children aged 3 to 9 · Android · In development · No ads

The Phoenix has lost its seven feathers, and with them the colour has drained out of the valley. Children help it find them again, place by place, with a friendly robin as their guide. Each place has its own small games: counting, sorting, adding and more, and the games grow with the child, from first numbers for three-year-olds to bigger sums for older children.

  • Eight places to visit, from the Meadow to a nest in the clouds.
  • Spoken words and captions, so children who can't read yet can play.
  • Up to four players on one device, each with their own progress.

The Meadow is free. A single one-time purchase opens the other seven places, with no subscriptions and no ads. The game collects no personal information: privacy policy.
