Seven Feathers: Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

In short: Seven Feathers does not collect or share any personal information. Players' names and progress stay on the device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Seven Feathers is made by ROPH Studios, the trading name of Tobias Backer Dirks, an independent developer based in Cyprus. You can reach us at hello@rophstudios.com.

Information we collect

None. The game has no sign-in and does not ask for an email address, photos, location, voice recordings or any other personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party tracking code.

What stays on the device

Up to four players can share the game. For each one it saves, on the device only: the name or nickname typed in for them, the picture they chose, their age group, and their progress (which feathers they have found and how hard the maths games are). It also saves the music, sound-effect and voice volume, whether captions are on, and whether the full game has been unlocked. None of this leaves the device. A grown-up can change or remove a player in the game's grown-ups area, and deleting the app removes everything.

Purchases

The Meadow is free, and the rest of the game can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase, including payment, is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so that it can unlock the content; we do not receive your payment details. Purchases are only possible in the grown-ups area, behind a grown-up check so that a child cannot start one alone.

Internet access

The game asks for internet access only so it can talk to Google Play for the purchase and for restoring it. Everything else, including all the spoken words, works offline.

Children

The game is made for children. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either. It contains no ads. The only way out of the app, the link to this privacy policy, sits behind the same grown-up check as purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
